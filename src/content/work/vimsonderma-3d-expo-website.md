## The brief

Vimsonderma Pharmaceuticals, a contract manufacturer of cosmetic and personal care products, exhibited at the CMPL expo in Mumbai, a large trade show for contract manufacturing and private label brands. They wanted something on the stand that would stop buyers walking past, explain the breadth of what they can manufacture, and turn a conversation into a lead the sales team could follow up after the show.

## What we built

We designed and built an interactive product configurator that ran full-screen in a browser on a large touchscreen mounted in the middle of the stand, directly beneath the illuminated signage for their care categories.

The flow is simple enough to finish in under two minutes while standing:

1. **Welcome and name.** An animated 3D guide character, Dr. Vimy, greets the visitor and asks for their name, so every later screen addresses them personally.
2. **Care category.** Face care, body care, hair care, baby care, men's range or sun care, each with a one-line description.
3. **Product type.** For example, within the men's range: face wash, body wash, serum, soap, beard oil or hair styling wax.
4. **Packaging in 3D.** The chosen product appears as a rotatable 3D model (for a beard oil, a glass dropper bottle). Visitors drag to rotate, pick a colour from the palette or choose a custom finish.
5. **Ingredients.** Key actives for the product, such as argan oil, jojoba oil and vitamin E for a beard oil.
6. **Saved concept and handoff.** A thank-you screen summarises the concept (category, product, packaging, ingredients), confirms that the team will be in touch, and shows a WhatsApp QR code so the visitor can start the conversation immediately. The concept and contact details are stored for the sales team, and the screen resets for the next visitor.

Building for the browser rather than as an installed application meant it ran on the stand's screen, on tablets carried by the team and on visitors' own phones, with nothing to install and nothing to break on the day.

## Design and engineering notes

- **The guide character does the talking.** Trade-show visitors will not read paragraphs. Dr. Vimy's short prompts at each step carried the brand's voice and kept people moving through the flow without staff standing over them.
- **Real-time 3D on ordinary hardware.** The packaging step uses browser-based 3D so the bottle rotates smoothly on the stand's display and on mid-range phones. Models and textures were kept small so the app loaded quickly on conference Wi-Fi.
- **Every interaction is a lead.** Instead of a business-card box, the configurator captures a named visitor, what they want to make and how to reach them, and hands them to WhatsApp, where the sales team already works.
- **Resilient for a full day of use.** The app resets itself between visitors, handles a dropped connection without losing a saved concept, and was tested on the actual stand hardware before the event.

## The result

In the client's own words, the impact was immediate. The configurator raised their presence at the event, contributed to sales and expanded their reach, and the quality of the experience helped position the company strongly in its market.
