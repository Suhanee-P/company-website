## The brief

Morphology Skincare is a founder-led skincare brand. The founders needed more than a website: they needed help deciding how to position the brand, a site that presented the products cleanly and made buying easy, and practical direction on social media so the brand could grow without a large marketing team.

## What we did

We worked with the founders on the market strategy first, because a website built before positioning is settled usually has to be rebuilt. With the audience and message agreed, we designed and built a clean product website focused on the range and on getting a visitor to a purchase decision with as little friction as possible.

Alongside the site, we gave the founders concrete social media guidance: what to post, how often, on which channels, and how to keep the brand consistent between the website and the feed.

## The result

The co-founder describes it as a collaboration where everything clicked. The site and the strategy work gave the brand a clear front door, and the social guidance gave the founders a plan they could execute themselves.
