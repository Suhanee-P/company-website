## The brief

Kshama Bhakta is a certified yoga instructor and physiotherapist with a clear idea of how she wanted to help people beyond one-to-one sessions. She needed a team that would not just take a feature list and code it, but would work through the product with her: what the app should do, for whom, and in what order.

## What we did

The collaboration ran over several months. It started with product ideation, shaping her expertise and vision into a concept that could be built and used, and continued into design and mobile development with regular releases. Because the app sits in the health and lifestyle space, we paid attention to how personal information is handled and to keeping the app's guidance within the boundaries a practitioner is comfortable with.

## The result

In her words, the team went beyond writing code and helped shape her vision into something she is genuinely proud of.
